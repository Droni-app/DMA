# Inglés 9°

- **Grado:** 9° (noveno)
- **Slug:** `ingles-9`
- **Intensidad sugerida:** 3 h/semana
- **Referentes:** Guía 22 (A2+), DBA Inglés 9°.

## Descripción

El curso enseña a describir culturas y costumbres, plantear hipótesis, explicar procesos y reportar información. Cierra con el ensayo de opinión y el debate.

## Lecciones

### Unidad 1 · Cultures and traditions
1. *Used to*
2. Relative clauses: *who, which, that*
3. Describing cultures
4. Colombian traditions and festivals
5. Writing a descriptive paragraph

### Unidad 2 · Technology and choices
6. Modals of possibility: *may, might*
7. Second conditional
8. Dilemmas and decisions
9. Technology in our lives
10. Giving advice and suggestions

### Unidad 3 · Processes and news
11. Passive voice: present
12. Passive voice: past
13. How things are made
14. Reported speech (introduction)
15. Reading the news

### Unidad 4 · Democracy and rights
16. Gerunds and infinitives
17. Human rights and democracy vocabulary
18. Reading strategies: skimming and scanning
19. Writing an opinion essay
20. Project: class debate
